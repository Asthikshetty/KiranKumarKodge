VISION & PLANNING

Kundapura Infrastructure & Development

Systematically driving key infrastructure, irrigation channels, and rail link projects for commuters and local industry.

01 / 04

The Long-Pending Kundapura Ring Road

Easing Decades of Traffic Congestion

The long-awaited ring road project, connecting Ferry Road to Sangam, is a top priority. After nearly 20 years, this crucial infrastructure is finally taking shape.

It is designed to link all major city roads, drastically decongest the town center, and streamline vehicular movement for residents and travelers alike.

02 / 04

Flyover at Sangam Circle on NH-66

A Critical Solution for Road Safety

With nine reported fatalities at this major intersection, a flyover is an urgent necessity.

Following a meeting with MP Kota Srinivas Poojary, the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) has been directed to prepare a proposal for a vehicular overpass at Sangam Circle to prevent further accidents and ensure the safety of commuters.

03 / 04

Varahi Right Bank Canal (Phase III)

Securing Water for Agriculture

The completion of the Varahi irrigation project is vital for the region's farming community.

With a revised cost of Rs. 178.50 crore, the project aims to irrigate 15,702 hectares in Udupi and Kundapura taluks. The MLA is consistently pushing for the timely completion and release of funds to ensure water reaches the fields.

04 / 04

Upgrading Kundapura Railway Station

Modernizing Rail Connectivity

A comprehensive upgrade of the Kundapura railway station is being pursued. This includes track doubling discussions, a demand for a new Padil bypass train between Kundapur and Bengaluru, and immediate local improvements.

Immediate steps include clearing overgrown grass on pedestrian pathways to improve passenger experience and safety.

GRASSROOTS WORKER

Meet Kiran Kumar Kodgi

Kiran Kumar Kodgi is an Indian politician, businessman, and a dedicated grassroots worker from the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP). Hailing from Baindur in Udupi district, he has been actively associated with the party for over three decades. A loyal follower and political heir of five-time MLA Halady Srinivas Shetty, he has earned the trust of the people through his accessibility and focus on resolving long-pending issues. Before his electoral debut, he was already a prominent community leader in the region's cooperative and cashew sectors.

"I have been in touch with booth-level party workers for three decades. I don't need any introduction either to the party workers or the people."

A statement from his first campaign, reflecting his deep-rooted, decades-long connection with the constituency.

"The existence of India, which is a fusion of rituals, traditions, and cultures, lies in religious practices. As long as religious rituals are held, the country can remain strong."

A statement from a cultural event, underscoring his belief in the importance of cultural and religious traditions.

"The path laid down by Narayana Guru, a pioneer of societal transformation, should serve as a guiding light for everyone."

A statement made while inaugurating the 170th birth anniversary of the social reformer, highlighting his commitment to social justice.
